
## OpticOdds official documentation

The official OpticOdds v3 guide describes a real-time odds aggregation API with normalized fixtures, odds snapshots, live SSE odds and results streams, results, bet grading, futures, injuries, historical odds, and player props. It states that access covers 100+ sportsbooks across 25+ sports, with up to five sportsbooks per request. The guide includes authenticated resource discovery, active fixtures, current odds, streaming updates, and results workflows. This is a provider capability description, not proof of SKYBET’s licensing or commercial entitlement; written permission, coverage for the intended Ghana-facing markets, and an operator agreement are still required before live connection.

Source: [OpticOdds Odds API: Getting Started Guide](https://developer.opticodds.com/docs/odds-api-getting-started-guide)

## Sportradar official documentation

Sportradar’s official getting-started documentation explicitly states that its APIs are a B2B service and are not intended to be called directly from a client application. The documentation exposes sport and league coverage areas, live odds, prematch, player props, futures, and a coverage matrix, but SKYBET would still need a commercial account, written rights, and server-side credentials. This reinforces the existing licensed-feed-first architecture: provider requests must remain on Railway, never in the browser.

Source: [Sportradar Developer: Get Started](https://developer.sportradar.com/getting-started/docs/get-started)

## The Odds API official product page

The official product page advertises more than 70 sports and more than 40 bookmakers, with soccer coverage including major European and Brazilian competitions, plus basketball, baseball, hockey, cricket, rugby, golf, tennis, and other markets. It offers head-to-head, spreads, totals, selected futures, and selected player props. The page lists a free 500-credit plan and paid monthly plans, but availability, bookmaker rights, and suitability for a Ghana-facing sportsbook must be confirmed commercially. It also advertises an Odds Widget, which may be useful as a reference or embeddable display concept, but SKYBET should use its own original UI and should not treat a widget as a substitute for backend ownership, licensing, or settlement controls.

Source: [The Odds API](https://the-odds-api.com/)

## Sportal365 official white-label and API-first offering

Sportal365 presents a customizable WordPress sports-news frontend connected to its CMS, live scores, odds, stats, branded widgets, and a cross-sport betslip that hands off to a sportsbook. It also advertises a headless, API-first route for operators who want to own the frontend. This makes it a potential content/data or white-label reference, but it is not evidence that SKYBET can use its odds or wagering engine without a commercial agreement. A white-label route would also reduce backend ownership and may not fit SKYBET’s existing first-party auth, manual payment review, Neon ledger, and admin-control architecture.

Source: [Sportal365 White Label](https://sportal365.com/white-label)

## BETBY official sportsbook offering

BETBY describes a managed B2B sportsbook covering more than 210,000 events per month, more than 125 sports, and over 3,000 betting markets, including virtual sports and esports. It advertises responsive customizable designs, several layouts, widgets, risk management, trading, segmentation, reporting, and a hosted integration model. This is the broadest event-count claim found in the reviewed official pages and is likely a better fit than a simple odds-only API if SKYBET wants a managed sportsbook engine and ready-made sportsbook presentation. The tradeoff is reduced ownership of the wagering backend and a commercial/operator agreement; it should be evaluated as a B2B platform integration, not copied as a frontend.

Source: [BETBY Sportsbook](https://betby.com/en/sportsbook/)

## OddsMatrix / EveryMatrix official odds-feed offering

EveryMatrix describes OddsMatrix as an end-to-end odds-feed solution with comprehensive pricing, live scores, rapid settlements, and content from multiple premium providers. Its official page claims 200,000+ live monthly betting events and 95% market uptime on high-demand football events, with content from providers including Kero, nVenue, and Beter. This appears to be a strong aggregator/managed-feed candidate for a broad sportsbook catalog, but it is enterprise B2B, requires a commercial agreement, and is not a drop-in free API or UI that SKYBET can simply copy.

Source: [EveryMatrix OddsMatrix Odds Feed](https://everymatrix.com/oddsmatrix/odds-feed/)
