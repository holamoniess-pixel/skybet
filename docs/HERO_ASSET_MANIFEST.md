# SKYBET Hero Asset Manifest

The homepage rotation reserves ten distinct, **brand-approved** 16:9 images. Every image should keep the left third visually quiet for headline and action contrast, contain no third-party sportsbook branding, and arrive with documented usage rights. The implemented manifest lives in `shared/heroAssets.ts` and contains these slots: stadium night, tunnel arrival, floodlit pitch, goal-net detail, crowd lightscape, match ball, training ground, city stadium, court action, and trophy room.

Current homepage artwork remains a temporary two-source composition rotation until the ten approved sources are supplied. The application does not represent those variants as ten unique images.
